Privacy policy

Last updated · 20 October 2026

Short version

We run the OCTA DEVS website and this team portal. We only collect what the product needs to work: team accounts, the CRM content your team enters, and conversations website visitors choose to start with us. We do not run ads, we do not sell data, and we do not attach hidden trackers to you.

What we collect

  • Team accounts. Name of record (email), a password (hashed — we never see it in readable form), a display username and a role that decides what you can do in the portal.
  • CRM content. The leads, contacts, deals and tasks your team writes into the portal.
  • Visitor conversations. When someone chats with us on the website, we receive their messages and any name or email they choose to give. Those conversations land in our team inbox so we can reply.
  • Technical basics. Standard server logs (such as request time and page) that hosted infrastructure keeps for security and stability. If a conversation happens on a particular page of the site, we note which page so we have context.

Cookies and local storage

The portal keeps it deliberately small:

  • Sign-in cookie sb-…-auth-token — strictly necessary. It is the session that keeps you signed in; without it the portal cannot know who you are. Removing it just signs you out.
  • Appearance — your dark/light preference is stored in your browser's local storage under octa-theme.
  • Consent choice — stored in your browser's local storage under octa-consent.

We use no advertising cookies, no analytics trackers and no third-party pixels. That is why the consent banner only asks about essentials: accepting or rejecting changes nothing about what is collected, because there is nothing else to collect.

Who sees your data

Team members see CRM content and visitor conversations according to the role their admins gave them. Super users manage accounts and roles. Visitor chat messages can also be forwarded as notifications into our own internal Discord channel so the team notices new enquiries faster — that channel is private to the OCTA DEVS team.

Where data lives

Sign-ins and data are handled by Supabase (authentication, managed Postgres database and file storage), and the portal itself is served by Vercel. Both are established providers with their own security practices. The website is served via Cloudflare.

Your choices

  • Team members: you can ask an owner to correct your username, change your role or delete your account at any time.
  • Visitors: say "forget my conversation" in the chat on the website and we will remove it from the inbox.
  • Everyone: clear the sign-in cookie or hit Sign out and your session ends on this device.

Changes

If this policy materially changes we update the date above. The live version on this page is always the one that counts.

Contact

Questions, deletion requests or anything else: message us through the chat on octadevs.fun or via the contact form on the site, and a human on the team will pick it up.
